Our story

We're pet people, first and forever

PawVille began with one groomer, one rescue dog and a big idea: professional grooming shouldn't feel scary for pets — or for you.

🎯

Our Mission

To make every grooming visit a calm, joyful and safe experience — with honest pricing, gentle handling and results your pet (and your sofa) will love.

🔭

Our Vision

A neighborhood where every pet has access to kind, professional care — and every pet parent can find the answer they need without jargon or judgment.

Who we are

A studio built on trust, training and treats

Since 2012 we've grown from a single grooming table into a full salon with day care, boarding and a mobile unit — without ever losing our small-studio heart.

Our journey

Milestones with muddy paws

2012

A one-table beginning

Founder Mia opens a two-chair studio with a rescue beagle named Biscuit as the first official client.

2016

Fear-free certified

Our whole team completes fear-free certification and we redesign the studio around low-stress handling.

2019

Day care & boarding open

A second building next door brings supervised playgroups, cozy suites and overnight care.

2022

The mobile van rolls out

Seniors, anxious pets and busy families can now enjoy the full salon experience at home.

2026

PawVille today

A 9-person team, 14k+ grooms completed, and the same rescue-dog philosophy we started with.
